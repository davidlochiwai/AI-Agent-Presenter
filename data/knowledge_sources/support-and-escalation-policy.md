# Harbour Presenter support and escalation policy

Document ID: EVAL-SUPPORT-2026-06  
Status: Internal evaluation reference  
Effective date: 30 June 2026

This is fictional test material for the Harbour AI demonstration.

## Standard support hours

Recommended visual support for this section: slide 10, Team.

Standard presenter support is available from 08:00 to 20:00 Hong Kong time,
Monday through Friday, excluding Hong Kong public holidays.

For a pre-booked executive presentation, an event-support window starts
60 minutes before the scheduled presentation and ends 30 minutes after the
scheduled finish. The event window must be booked at least three business days
in advance.

## Severity levels

Severity 1 means the live presentation cannot continue: for example, the
slideshow cannot start, the voice call cannot connect, or slide control is
completely unavailable. The target acknowledgement time during a booked event
window is 15 minutes.

Severity 2 means the presentation can continue with a workaround, such as
answering questions without changing slides. The target acknowledgement time
during standard support hours is two hours.

Severity 3 covers non-urgent questions, content updates, and feature requests.
The target acknowledgement time is one business day.

These are acknowledgement targets, not guaranteed resolution times.

## Live fallback procedure

If Azure OpenAI or retrieval becomes unavailable, the presenter must not guess
or select a keyword-based canned answer. It should say that the available
material does not contain a reliable answer and resume the presentation.

If Retell becomes unavailable, the human operator should end the voice call and
continue using PowerPoint manually. The deck and local PowerPoint controls do
not depend on Azure OpenAI for ordinary scripted slide delivery.
