# Harbour Presenter customer pilot retrospective

Document ID: EVAL-CUSTOMER-2026-06  
Status: Internal evaluation reference

This is fictional test material for the Harbour AI demonstration. It does not
describe a real company or customer.

## Harbour Bank pilot

Harbour Bank completed three full rehearsals before its first Cantonese
quarterly-results presentation. Each rehearsal used the final meeting-room
microphone and the same Windows presentation computer planned for the live
event.

The final rehearsal lasted 42 minutes. Two interruptions were deliberately
introduced to verify that the presenter could answer a question, show the
relevant supporting slide, and then return to the next unsaid script beat.

The pilot team consisted of twelve participants:

- four investor-relations users;
- three corporate-communications users;
- two information-security reviewers;
- two event-operations users;
- one Harbour AI solution engineer.

The bank approved the pilot after the third rehearsal. Contract value is not
included in this document and must not be inferred.

Recommended visual support for questions about this pilot: slide 6, Customer
Cases.

## Starline Group pilot

Starline Group used Harbour Presenter for an internal new-route announcement.
The pilot covered Cantonese delivery only. Live Cantonese-to-English switching
was not enabled because that capability remains scheduled for the first quarter
of 2027.

Starline requested a concise answer style: no more than three spoken sentences
for an audience interruption. The team also requested that the system stay on
the current slide when a supporting visual was not necessary.

Recommended visual support: slide 6 for the customer example and slide 8 for
questions about future language switching.

## Victoria Retail pilot

Victoria Retail tested the presenter in twelve city-manager meeting rooms. The
same approved deck and script were distributed to every city, but each room ran
its own local Windows presentation computer.

The pilot did not use one shared PowerPoint instance across all twelve cities.
Each room had an independent presentation session. This distinction is
important when answering questions about deployment scale.
